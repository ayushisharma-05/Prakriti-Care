Our Story

From Indore's Streets
to India's Heart

A grassroots movement that turned compassion into a tech-powered rescue network.

2019

The Spark in Indore

A group of animal lovers in Indore witnessed a stray dog die on the roadside because no vet could be reached in time. That night, Prakriti Care was conceived — not as an organisation, but as a promise.

2021

Expanding to Bilaspur

Our volunteer-led rescue network crossed state lines into Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, establishing the first multi-city stray animal ambulance coordination system in Central India.

2024

Active in 15+ Indian States

Today, 1,200+ trained citizen responders, 84 verified vet partners, and a real-time dispatch tracker form our backbone — connecting isolated animal lovers into one unified compassion network.

6+ Years Active
4,800+ Lives Saved

Simple 3-Step Process

How It Works

Our tech-enabled network ensures rapid medical response and transparent tracking for every reported case.

Step 1

Snap & Report Emergency

Upload a photo and pin the GPS coordinates of the injured animal through our mobile-friendly SOS portal.

Step 2

Local Indian Vet Network Alerted

Our system dispatches alerts to the nearest partner veterinarians and ambulance drivers in Indore & Bilaspur.

Step 3

Real-time Rescue Tracking

Track the dispatched rescue vehicle in real-time as they navigate to the location and administer emergency treatment.
